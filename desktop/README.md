# Kommandr

One board for you and your AI coding agents. Manage projects, issues, epics, and sprints from your Mac, and keep up from your iPhone or iPad.

[Download Kommandr](https://kommandr.com/download) · [Account and membership](https://kommandr.com/account) · [Release notes](./CHANGELOG.md)

Desktop installers are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Existing Kommandr installations receive updates through this directory's `latest-mac.yml` feed. iOS builds use the original `com.kommandr` Apple app and are available through TestFlight.

Kommandr releases are tagged `desktop-vX.Y.Z`. Hoblets is a separate product with its own app identifiers and update feed.
