# Hoblets

Your own little team. Talk to your bots, share their workspace, and open their desktops whenever you need to lend a hand.

## Download Hoblets for Mac

**[Download the new Hoblets app](https://hoblets.com/download)**

| Mac | Download |
|---|---|
| Apple Silicon (M1 and later) | [Download for Apple Silicon](https://hoblets.com/download/mac?arch=arm64) |
| Intel | [Download for Intel](https://hoblets.com/download/mac?arch=x64) |

Requires macOS 13 or later. Open the DMG and drag Hoblets to Applications.

If you installed Hoblets before version 0.2.0, install this new Hoblets app once. Your existing local sign-in data is preserved.

The app checks for updates automatically and shows what's new before you download. Restart whenever you're ready. Builds are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.
